# DrawSpace Generated Assets

**Generated assets are not integrated into the application.** No React, route, style, or functional code was changed. Existing `public/assets/social/og-drawspace-home.webp`, logo, favicon and inline SVG assets were preserved.

All generated isolated assets use transparent PNG backgrounds and the DrawSpace monochrome/cyan palette. Avatar, achievement and title images are 512 × 512; the app icon is 1024 × 1024; horizontal logos are 1200 × 300. They were generated as separate images and normalized to requested dimensions. They remain standalone files for future review; no app code references them.

## Verification notes

- 31 requested individual assets were created: 10 avatars, 3 brand graphics, 12 achievement icons and 6 title icons. The contact sheet is a 32nd image file. The brief's “TOTAL: 31 image files” conflicts with its itemized counts, which add up to 32 including the preview.
- All 31 individual transparent-background PNGs have alpha channels and exact requested dimensions. The contact sheet is opaque by design.
- Files are PNG and use black/white/grayscale with cyan accents. The master preview was visually reviewed for group contents and names.
- Some generated logo typography is small at the contact-sheet scale; the logo images are concept assets and should be reviewed at full resolution before any future adoption.
- Existing social preview remains unchanged.
- No generated assets were imported, referenced, or connected to the application.
